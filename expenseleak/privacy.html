Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Public beta notice. ExpenseLeak AI is currently being prepared as a public beta. Paid access remains disabled until billing, entitlement, legal-operator and end-to-end launch checks are completed.

What data we process

The free CSV preview runs locally in your browser. If you create an account and choose secure workspace features, ExpenseLeak may process your account email, workspace records, uploaded business documents, transactions, vendors, contracts, procurement records, support requests, connector metadata and audit events needed to provide the service.

Uploaded files

Private workspace files are stored in private storage and are not part of the public demo. Do not upload passwords, full payment-card numbers, government IDs, medical records or other information that is not necessary for business-spend analysis.

Connected services

When a supported connector is enabled, authorization tokens are kept server-side. ExpenseLeak requests only the scopes needed for the selected integration. A connector is not treated as connected until the provider authorization succeeds.

How data is used

Data is used to operate the workspace, analyze business spend, identify items for review, support procurement and renewal workflows, provide requested integrations, maintain security and respond to support requests. Illustrative public demo data is not customer data.

AI and automated analysis

ExpenseLeak may use automated analysis to extract or classify information and highlight possible duplicates, price changes, overlap, unused licenses or other review opportunities. These are decision-support signals, not guaranteed savings or instructions to cancel a service.

Retention and deletion

Workspace data is retained while needed to provide the beta service and for reasonable security, audit and recovery purposes. You can request deletion through the Support & Data Requests page. Deletion can be limited by legal, security or fraud-prevention obligations.

Security

ExpenseLeak uses authentication, private storage, row-level access controls, server-side secret storage, signed webhooks and other safeguards. No online service can promise absolute security. See the Security page for more detail.

Service providers

The beta currently relies on infrastructure providers including GitHub Pages and Supabase. Additional integration providers are used only when you connect them.

International processing

Service providers may process data in countries different from your own. The product is being prepared for broader commercial launch; before paid launch, the operator will publish the final controller/business identity and any required regional notices.

Your requests

For access, correction, deletion, privacy or security requests, use Support & Data Requests. Do not send sensitive credentials in a support message.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the beta, billing and integrations develop. The date at the top will change when material updates are published.
