2026 SUMP PUMP COST GUIDE

How much does sump pump installation cost?

For many U.S. homes, current 2026 consumer cost guides place professional sump pump installation around $800 to $1,500. A straightforward replacement can be less complex than a first-time installation, while projects that require a new pit, extensive drainage, electrical work or backup systems can reach several thousand dollars.

New installation versus replacement

A replacement may reuse the existing pit, discharge line and electrical setup. A new system can require concrete cutting, excavation, a basin, drainage connections, a discharge route and sometimes electrical upgrades. That difference is why two “sump pump” quotes can be far apart.

Common pump types

  • Submersible: sits inside the pit and is common in finished or frequently used basements because it is compact and generally quieter.
  • Pedestal: keeps the motor above the pit and can be easier to service, though the visible equipment takes more space.
  • Battery backup: adds protection when utility power fails and is often priced separately from the primary pump.
  • Water-powered backup: available in some homes where plumbing and local rules make it appropriate.

What changes the installation price

Pump capacity, pit depth, concrete cutting, drainage layout, discharge distance, check valves, electrical outlets, alarms, battery backup, permits and difficult access can all affect the total. Crawl-space installations can be more labor intensive because excavation and material handling may need to be done by hand.

How sump pumps fit into waterproofing

A sump pump manages collected water; it does not by itself fix every source of basement moisture. If water is entering through walls, grading, cracks or hydrostatic pressure, the full repair may include drainage or waterproofing work in addition to the pump.

For broader planning, see our basement waterproofing cost guide and waterproofing cost per square foot.

Questions to ask before approving the work

  • Is this a replacement or a completely new pit and drainage system?
  • What pump capacity are you recommending and why?
  • Is the discharge line included, and where will it terminate?
  • Are electrical work, permits, alarms and backup power included?
  • What maintenance schedule and warranty apply?
