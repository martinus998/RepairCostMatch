How RepairCostMatch may make money.

Last updated: September 17, 2026

RepairCostMatch is intended to be free for homeowners to use. The site may earn compensation from contractor marketplaces, home-service companies or other referral partners when a visitor clicks through, requests a match, becomes a qualified lead or completes a project.

Partner coverage

A partner relationship does not mean every contractor in a ZIP code or every available repair option is represented. RepairCostMatch should not claim to search the entire market unless that claim can be substantiated.

Sponsored placement

If compensation affects the placement of a contractor or service result, that placement should be clearly labeled as sponsored or otherwise disclosed so it is not mistaken for an independent ranking.

Cost guidance stays separate

Educational cost ranges are not live contractor quotes. Compensation from a referral partner does not turn a planning estimate into a verified price.

No added homeowner fee

RepairCostMatch does not currently charge homeowners a separate fee to use the public planning preview. Contractors or partners determine their own project pricing and terms.
