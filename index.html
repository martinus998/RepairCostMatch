PLANNING RANGE, NOT A QUOTE

Foundation repair often lands in the low-to-mid thousands — but the repair method matters more than the headline average.

Major 2026 consumer cost guides place many foundation repairs around $2,200–$8,200 nationally, while serious stabilization, excavation, drainage work or multiple piers can cost much more. Waterproofing ranges also vary sharply by method, size and whether work is interior or exterior.

Minor crack sealing / localized repairLower-cost tier
Drainage / waterproofing workMid-range to high
Settlement stabilization / piersOften higher
Major structural correctionCan exceed averages

Costs are educational planning ranges only. Your actual price depends on location, soil, access, severity, engineering needs and the contractor's scope.
