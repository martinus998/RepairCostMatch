2026 CRAWL SPACE GUIDE

How much does a crawl space sump pump cost?

A crawl-space sump pump project can be a simple pump-and-pit installation or part of a larger drainage and moisture-control system. The total depends on where water collects, how it reaches the pit, where discharge goes and whether electrical or backup-power work is included.

What can be included in the quote

  • Sump pit: excavation, basin size and location can affect labor.
  • Primary pump: capacity and pump type should match the expected water load.
  • Drainage connection: perimeter drains or channels may feed water to the pit.
  • Discharge line: routing water safely away from the foundation can add material and labor.
  • Electrical work: a dedicated outlet or other electrical changes may be required.
  • Backup system: battery or secondary-pump options can be priced separately.

Why the water source matters

A sump pump manages collected water; it does not automatically correct grading, roof runoff, plumbing leaks or high crawl-space humidity. Ask the contractor to explain where the water is entering and why the proposed drainage path sends it to the pump.

Compare the pump with the surrounding system

Some proposals combine the pump with a vapor barrier, drainage mat, encapsulation or dehumidifier. Separate those line items so you can compare equal scopes. Our vapor barrier cost guide, encapsulation cost guide and dehumidifier cost guide explain those components.

Questions to ask before installation

  • Where is the water coming from?
  • What drainage feeds the sump pit?
  • Where does the discharge line end?
  • Is electrical work included?
  • What pump capacity is specified?
  • Is backup power included or optional?
  • What maintenance is required?

For the broader moisture-control picture, see our crawl space moisture repair cost guide. If you are comparing a basement installation instead, use our general sump pump installation guide.

Standing water near electrical equipment can be hazardous. The cause of water intrusion and the correct drainage design require an on-site evaluation.
