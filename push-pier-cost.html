2026 COST GUIDE

How much do push piers cost?

Push piers are a structural foundation-support method often considered when an existing foundation has settled and the structure provides enough weight for the installation process. Quotes are commonly priced per pier, but the total depends on the number of support locations, depth, footing condition, access, engineering and whether controlled lifting is included.

Recent 2026 cost references commonly place installed residential push piers in a broad planning range around $1,500 to $3,500 per pier. Whole projects frequently reach five figures because several piers may be needed.

What is included in a push pier quote?

A complete scope may include excavation at the footing, steel brackets, pier sections, hydraulic driving, load transfer, elevation measurements, backfill and cleanup. Engineering, permits, drainage work and finish repairs may be separate.

Why pier count matters so much

A proposal for six piers and a proposal for twelve piers are fundamentally different repairs even if the per-pier price is similar. Ask how the contractor selected each pier location and what measurements support the count.

Push piers vs. helical piers

Push piers use the weight of the structure during installation, while helical piers are advanced with screw-like helix plates and are designed around torque and load criteria. Neither system is automatically better. The correct choice depends on the structure, soil, footing, access and engineering design.

Questions to ask before approving the work

  • How many piers are included and why?
  • What installation resistance or depth criteria will be used?
  • Is stabilization only planned, or will lifting be attempted?
  • Are engineering and permits included?
  • What happens if the footing is damaged or the required depth is greater than expected?
  • Does the warranty transfer to a future owner?
