PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy before contractor matching.

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Current preview

The current RepairCostMatch planning tool runs in your browser. It may ask about the visible home problem, perceived severity, foundation type and ZIP code. Those answers are used to produce the educational planning result shown on screen. The current preview does not submit those answers to a contractor, marketplace or referral partner.

No contact information required to start

The current planning flow does not require your name, email address or phone number. Do not enter Social Security numbers, payment-card information, account credentials, medical information or other sensitive identifiers into the preview.

Future partner referrals

If RepairCostMatch later connects to a contractor-matching or home-services partner, the product will explain what information is being shared and ask for appropriate consent before sending a repair profile or contact information. Any separate marketing, call or SMS consent must be presented clearly rather than hidden in the basic planning flow.

Analytics

Analytics may be added to measure page visits and product interactions. Repair-profile details such as ZIP code, specific home symptoms and contact information should not be sent as analytics event parameters. This notice will be updated before any analytics or advertising technology materially changes how data is handled.

Security

Private partner credentials, API keys and lead-routing secrets must remain on secure server-side systems and must not be placed in public website code. If persistent personal information is collected later, RepairCostMatch intends to use access controls, retention limits and a deletion process appropriate to the integration.

Changes

This notice will change as the service adds contractor matching, analytics or other features. Material changes should be published before the relevant feature is used for live customer data.
