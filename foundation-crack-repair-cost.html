2026 CRACK REPAIR COST GUIDE

How much does foundation crack repair cost?

For a simple, non-structural foundation crack, current 2026 consumer cost guides commonly show professional repair around $250 to $800 per crack. That number can be useful for planning, but it does not apply to every crack.

If the crack is related to settlement, wall movement, bowing or another structural condition, the repair can move into the thousands or tens of thousands because the contractor may be stabilizing the foundation rather than simply sealing the crack.

Non-structural crack repair

Stable vertical or diagonal cracks in poured concrete may sometimes be repaired with epoxy or polyurethane injection, sealing or patching. The goal may be to stop water entry, bond the crack or protect the concrete rather than correct structural movement. For a dedicated scope comparison, see our basement wall crack injection cost guide.

Structural crack repair

Horizontal cracks, stair-step cracks, displacement across a crack, active widening or cracking combined with sloping floors and sticking doors can point to a larger problem. Repairs may involve wall reinforcement, anchors, underpinning, piers, drainage correction or other stabilization work.

For pattern-specific guidance, read our foundation crack warning-sign guide and horizontal foundation crack guide.

What changes the price

  • Crack type and movement: a stable shrinkage crack is different from active structural movement.
  • Water intrusion: leaks may require drainage or waterproofing in addition to crack repair.
  • Foundation type: poured concrete, block, slab and crawl-space foundations can require different approaches.
  • Access: finished basements, tight crawl spaces and exterior excavation can add labor.
  • Underlying cause: soil movement or settlement may require stabilization rather than only sealing.

Questions to ask a contractor

  • Do you believe the crack is structural or non-structural, and why?
  • Is the crack currently moving?
  • What repair method are you proposing?
  • Does the price include water-control or drainage work?
  • Would an independent structural-engineering opinion be useful before a major repair?

2026 source notes

HomeGuide lists many non-structural foundation crack repairs around $250–$800 per crack and much higher costs when structural repair is involved. HomeAdvisor and This Old House likewise show that small crack repairs sit near the low end of foundation work, while settlement and stabilization can be substantially more expensive.
