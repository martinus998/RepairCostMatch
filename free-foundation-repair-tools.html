A better order for comparing foundation repair

  1. Identify the symptom. Cracks, settlement, water and bowing walls can lead to very different repair scopes.
  2. Estimate the budget category. Use a broad calculator before anchoring on one contractor price.
  3. Read the written proposal. Confirm method, quantity, engineering, permits and exclusions.
  4. Normalize the math. For pier projects, compare unit price, pier count and fixed project costs separately.
  5. Verify independently when appropriate. Large or conflicting structural proposals may justify an independent professional evaluation.

What these tools do not do

They do not diagnose a foundation, choose a structural repair method or replace an inspection. Local soil, access, labor, engineering, permits and hidden conditions can move a real contractor price outside any online range.

Educational planning only. Rapid movement, unstable walls, flooding, gas or electrical hazards and other immediate safety concerns require appropriate local professional evaluation.
