2026 ENGINEERING COST GUIDE

How much does a structural engineer cost for a foundation?

Current 2026 consumer guidance commonly places many independent structural-engineer foundation assessments around $400 to $700. A simple site visit can sit near the lower end, while larger homes, calculations, stamped plans, detailed written reports or return visits can cost more.

What the fee may include

  • Site inspection: visual review, measurements and discussion of symptoms such as cracks, wall movement or uneven floors.
  • Written report: some engagements include a formal summary while others price documentation separately.
  • Calculations or repair design: engineering drawings, specifications or stamped plans can add cost beyond a basic assessment.
  • Follow-up: contractor questions, permit support or a second visit may be separate line items.

Why homeowners pay for an independent opinion

Foundation contractors can be valuable for pricing and installing repairs, but they also sell the repair system. When a proposal is expensive or several contractors recommend different solutions, an independent engineer can help clarify whether movement is structural and what kind of stabilization is appropriate.

For the broader inspection decision, compare our foundation inspection cost guide. If you already have repair bids, use the foundation repair cost guide to compare scope rather than only totals.

What changes the engineering price

Home size, foundation type, crawl-space access, number of problem areas, complexity of movement, travel, local professional rates and the level of documentation all affect the fee. A stamped repair design is a different service from a brief diagnostic visit.

Questions to ask before booking

  • Is the fee for inspection only or does it include a written report?
  • Are measurements, calculations or drawings included?
  • Will the engineer recommend a repair method or only document conditions?
  • Is a follow-up visit included after contractor work begins?
  • Are permit or plan-review services priced separately?
