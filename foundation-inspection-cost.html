2026 INSPECTION COST GUIDE

How much does a foundation inspection cost?

Current 2026 consumer cost guides commonly place a standard professional foundation inspection around $300 to $600, while an independent structural-engineer foundation assessment is often around $400 to $700. More complex homes, larger foundations and detailed written reports can cost more.

Why inspection prices vary

  • Foundation type: slabs may be faster to review than pier-and-beam, crawl-space or complex basement systems.
  • Home size: larger structures take longer to inspect.
  • Access: tight crawl spaces, finished basements or obstructed exterior walls can increase inspection time.
  • Documentation: a verbal evaluation is different from a detailed stamped or written engineering report.
  • Travel and local rates: engineering and inspection costs vary substantially by market.

Contractor inspection versus structural engineer

Some foundation contractors offer free or low-cost evaluations because they also sell repair work. That can be useful for estimating a proposed solution, but an independent structural engineer may be worth considering when the recommended repair is expensive, when multiple contractors disagree, or when you want a diagnosis from someone who is not selling the repair itself. For a focused fee breakdown, see our structural engineer foundation cost guide.

When an inspection can save money

A few hundred dollars spent clarifying the problem can be small compared with a multi-thousand-dollar stabilization project. An inspection can help separate cosmetic cracking, drainage problems and true structural movement before bids are compared.

If the house shows uneven floors, sticking doors or widening cracks, see our foundation settling signs guide. For repair budgeting, use our foundation repair cost guide.

Questions to ask before booking

  • Who will perform the inspection and what are their qualifications?
  • Will I receive a written report?
  • Does the fee include measurements, photos or repair recommendations?
  • Is the inspector financially connected to the repair company?
  • Will follow-up questions or a second site visit cost extra?
