How much do helical piers cost?

Helical piers are one method used to stabilize or support structures when settlement or load conditions require deeper support. Pricing is often discussed per pier, but the total project cost depends on how many piers are specified, how deep they must be installed, access around the home, engineering requirements and whether lifting or related repairs are included.

Recent 2026 market guides commonly show installed residential helical pier pricing in a broad range around $1,500 to $4,000 per pier, with many standard residential installations clustering closer to the lower-middle part of that range. Complex sites, deeper installation and heavier loads can cost more.

Why cost per pier can be misleading

A quote for eight piers at one unit price is not directly comparable with a quote for twelve piers at another price unless both contractors are proposing the same load path, spacing, bracket system, depth criteria, engineering scope and lifting plan.

Common cost drivers

  • Depth and soil: deeper bearing conditions require more shaft sections and installation time.
  • Pier count: the number of support points can change the project total dramatically.
  • Access: tight side yards, decks, landscaping or interior access can increase labor.
  • Engineering: structural design, load requirements and permit documentation may be separate line items.
  • Lifting: some projects stabilize the structure in place while others attempt controlled lift, which can add complexity.

Helical piers vs. push piers

Both systems can be used for foundation support, but they are not interchangeable in every situation. The appropriate method depends on structural loads, soil conditions, access and the repair design. Ask the contractor or engineer why the proposed system fits your property rather than choosing based only on unit price.

Questions to ask before signing

  • How many piers are included and why?
  • What installation depth or torque criteria will be used?
  • Is engineering included?
  • Does the quote include excavation, brackets, lifting, backfill and restoration?
  • What happens if deeper installation is required?
  • How is the warranty structured and what does it actually cover?

A low per-pier number can become expensive if the proposal uses more piers, excludes engineering or adds restoration later. Compare complete scopes. Our foundation repair cost per pier guide explains how to read unit pricing, while the foundation repair cost guide gives broader planning context.
