TERMS OF USE

Planning information, not a contractor quote.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Educational service

RepairCostMatch provides general educational information about home repair symptoms, possible project categories, DIY suitability screening, cost planning, quote comparison and contractor-selection questions. It does not inspect your property and cannot determine the actual cause, safety status, required repair method or final price of a project.

Not a contractor, engineer or inspector

RepairCostMatch is not a home-improvement contractor, structural engineer, building inspector, architect or other licensed construction professional. Information on the site is not a substitute for an on-site evaluation when one is appropriate.

AI Repair Assistant

The Pro AI Repair Assistant generates educational responses from the question you enter and the repair profile available in the current session. AI output can be incomplete, inaccurate or unsuitable for conditions that cannot be observed online. You must use independent judgment, follow product instructions and local requirements, and obtain qualified professional help when structural movement, electricity, gas, sewage, active flooding, excavation, instability or another significant hazard may be involved.

The AI Repair Assistant is intentionally designed to avoid step-by-step structural repair instructions for higher-risk conditions. A refusal or professional-first recommendation is part of the product's safety design and does not indicate that the property has been diagnosed.

Screening scores and DIY suitability

A displayed 0–100 risk screening score is a planning signal derived from information you report. It is not a percentage of damage, probability of failure, structural rating, inspection result or engineering assessment. DIY suitability labels are conservative planning guidance and do not guarantee that work is safe or code-compliant.

Cost ranges are not quotes

Any displayed dollar range is an educational planning estimate based on broad project categories. Actual prices vary by location, material, access, soil, structure, damage severity, permits, engineering, labor, contractor scope and other factors. A contractor or other qualified professional must provide the actual estimate or quote.

Quote checker limitations

The quote tools compare amounts you enter against broad planning benchmarks. They do not verify a contractor's diagnosis, scope, price, license, warranty or workmanship.

Provider information

Local provider information may come from connected directory sources. Availability, ratings, review counts, phone numbers, websites and other fields can change. License, insurance and provider-specific pricing are shown only when separately supported by an available source. RepairCostMatch does not guarantee provider workmanship or the lowest available price.

Urgent conditions

If you believe a structure may be unsafe, movement is rapid, utilities are affected, flooding is active or another immediate hazard exists, do not rely on this site to determine urgency. Contact an appropriate local professional or emergency service.

Availability and limits

AI and provider features depend on third-party services and may be rate limited, interrupted or temporarily unavailable. RepairCostMatch may change reasonable usage limits to protect reliability and cost.

Partner relationships

RepairCostMatch may receive compensation from contractor marketplaces, home-service providers or referral partners for clicks, leads or completed projects. Compensation does not convert an educational estimate into a contractor quote.
