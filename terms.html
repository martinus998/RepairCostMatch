TERMS OF USE

Planning information, not a contractor quote.

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Educational service

RepairCostMatch provides general educational information about home repair symptoms, possible project categories, cost planning, quote comparison and contractor-selection questions. It does not inspect your property and cannot determine the actual cause, safety status, repair method or final price of a project.

Not a contractor, engineer or inspector

RepairCostMatch is not a home-improvement contractor, structural engineer, building inspector, architect or other licensed construction professional. Information on the site is not a substitute for an on-site evaluation when one is appropriate.

Cost ranges are not quotes

Any displayed dollar range is an educational planning estimate based on broad project categories. Actual prices vary by location, material, access, soil, structure, damage severity, permits, engineering, labor, contractor scope and other factors. A contractor or other qualified professional must provide the actual estimate or quote.

Quote checker limitations

The “Is this quote fair?” tool compares an amount you enter against RepairCostMatch's broad planning benchmark for the selected repair category and severity. It does not verify whether a contractor's diagnosis, scope, price, license, warranty or workmanship is correct. A quote below the planning band may omit important work, and a quote above it may include legitimate additional scope. The current version does not apply a ZIP-level local market multiplier.

No guarantee of contractor coverage or price

If contractor matching is enabled later, RepairCostMatch may work with a limited partner network and cannot promise that every local contractor, every repair option or the lowest available price is included.

Urgent conditions

If you believe a structure may be unsafe, movement is rapid, utilities are affected, flooding is active or another immediate hazard exists, do not rely on this site to determine urgency. Contact an appropriate local professional or emergency service.

Partner relationships

RepairCostMatch may receive compensation from contractor marketplaces, home-service providers or referral partners for clicks, leads or completed projects. Compensation does not convert an educational estimate into a contractor quote.

Availability

The site may change, pause or remove preview features while integrations and data sources are developed. We do not promise uninterrupted availability or that every guide remains current forever.
