Foundation repair quote red flags checklist

This checklist is not a contractor blacklist. A missing item is a reason to ask a question before signing. The goal is to make the written proposal clear enough that you can compare scope, not just price.

Open interactive quote checker

The diagnosis is vague

The proposal names a repair method but does not explain what condition it is meant to correct or what evidence supports the diagnosis.

Quantities are missing

Piers, anchors, beams, drain length, wall area or other major units are not counted or located in writing.

The goal is unclear

The proposal does not distinguish stabilization, lifting, waterproofing, cosmetic repair or a combination of goals.

Engineering and permits are not assigned

The contract does not state who is responsible for engineering, permits, inspections or related costs.

Drainage is discussed but excluded

Water or soil conditions are part of the diagnosis, but the written scope does not address who handles drainage corrections.

Restoration is not defined

Concrete, landscaping, drywall, flooring, excavation, cleanup or debris removal could be needed but are not included or excluded clearly.

The deposit or payment milestones are unclear

The contract does not state the deposit, when remaining payments become due, or what work milestone triggers payment.

The warranty is only verbal

Coverage, duration, transfer terms and exclusions are discussed but not supplied in writing.

Same-day pressure replaces explanation

A major structural contract is presented as an immediate decision without enough time to understand the written scope or seek another opinion.

Contractors diagnose different problems

One contractor recommends piers, another drainage and another wall stabilization. Clarify the diagnosis before treating the prices as comparable.

What to do when you check several boxes

Use the interactive quote checker to turn missing items into questions for the contractor. If the project is expensive or diagnoses conflict, consider the second opinion guide. If the main concern is price, use Is My Foundation Repair Quote Too High?.
