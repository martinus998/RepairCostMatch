ABOUT REPAIRCOSTMATCH

RepairCostMatch helps homeowners understand the repair before they hire.

RepairCostMatch is a U.S.-focused educational planning tool for homeowners trying to make sense of foundation and basement-water problems before a sales call or site visit.

No phone number to startPlanning ranges, not quotesTransparent sources

What RepairCostMatch does

We turn common homeowner symptoms into a clearer planning path: what category of repair may be involved, what can affect cost, and what questions to ask a qualified local professional.

What RepairCostMatch does not do

We do not inspect homes, diagnose structural conditions, issue engineering opinions, guarantee prices, or claim to compare every contractor in a market.

Our standards

Transparent estimatesEvery range is labeled as educational planning, never a contractor quote.
Visible sourcingWe publish the date and sources behind headline cost guidance.
No fabricated proofWe do not use invented reviews, fake contractor quotes, or fake expert endorsements.
Privacy firstThe current planner works without a phone number and keeps the repair profile in the browser.
Safety over conversionPotentially dangerous movement or flooding should be professionally evaluated rather than treated as a web-estimate problem.
Corrections welcomeIf a source changes or guidance is unclear, we want it reported and corrected.
