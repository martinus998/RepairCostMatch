2026 CRAWL SPACE COST GUIDE

How much does crawl space mold remediation cost?

Current 2026 consumer cost guides commonly place crawl-space mold cleanup around $500 to $4,000, although a small localized area can cost less and widespread contamination, difficult access or damaged building materials can cost much more.

What the remediation quote may include

  • Inspection or testing: some projects begin with a professional assessment or sampling.
  • Containment: the work area may need to be isolated from the living space.
  • Removal and cleaning: affected insulation, debris or porous materials may need removal before surfaces are cleaned or treated.
  • Drying: fans, dehumidification or other drying work may be part of the project.
  • Disposal and restoration: removed insulation, wood or other materials may require replacement after cleanup.

Mold cleanup and moisture repair are not the same scope

Remediation addresses existing mold growth. It does not automatically solve the reason the crawl space became damp. Leaks, groundwater, poor drainage, exposed soil or high humidity can remain after cleanup unless they are corrected separately.

For the water-control side, compare our crawl space moisture repair cost guide, crawl space drainage cost and crawl space dehumidifier cost.

Structural damage can change the total

If moisture has damaged joists, beams, rim joists, sill plates or supports, ask for structural repairs to be priced separately from remediation. A lower cleanup quote can still become a larger project if wood replacement or leveling is required.

Questions to ask before hiring

  • What area is included in the remediation scope?
  • Is testing or clearance testing included?
  • What materials will be removed?
  • How will the crawl space be dried afterward?
  • What is being done to stop the moisture from returning?
  • Are structural repairs included or separate?
