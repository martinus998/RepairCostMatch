CRAWL SPACE QUOTE REVIEW

Does your crawl space encapsulation quote seem too high?

Encapsulation proposals can bundle very different amounts of work. One may include only a vapor barrier and sealing, while another adds drainage, dehumidification, insulation, mold cleanup or structural repairs.

Break the quote into components

  • Ground and wall vapor barrier.
  • Sealing vents, penetrations and access doors.
  • Drainage or sump work.
  • Dehumidifier and electrical requirements.
  • Insulation removal or replacement.
  • Mold remediation or wood repair.

Compare material and coverage details

Ask what surfaces are covered, how seams are sealed, how material is attached, what happens at piers and walls, and what warranty applies. The word “encapsulation” alone does not define the scope.

Separate moisture control from structural repair

Rotten joists, beams, sill plates or support posts are separate repair categories. If they are included, compare those items independently using the crawl space repair guide.

Check whether drainage is solving a real water problem

If standing water or repeated runoff is present, drainage may be appropriate. If the issue is mainly humidity, the scope may be different. Ask the contractor to explain what condition each line item is addressing.

Frequently asked questions

Why are crawl space encapsulation quotes so different?

Proposals may include different barrier materials, wall coverage, sealing, drainage, dehumidification, insulation, mold work and structural repairs.

Is a dehumidifier always included?

No. Some encapsulation systems include one and others price it separately. Check the equipment, drainage and electrical scope.

Should mold remediation be part of encapsulation?

It may be a separate item when mold or contaminated materials are present. Ask for cleanup scope and moisture-control work to be listed clearly.
