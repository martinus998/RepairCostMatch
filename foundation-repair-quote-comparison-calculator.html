Why quote totals can be far apart

Two bids can differ because the contractors diagnose different problems, propose different repair areas, use different quantities or include different project-level work. A lower total may reflect a smaller but appropriate scope, or it may exclude work that another bid includes. A higher total may reflect a larger necessary scope, or simply more included items. The number alone does not tell you which explanation is correct.

Frequently asked questions

Should I pick the lowest quote?

Not from price alone. First confirm that the bids address the same diagnosis and comparable written scope.

Why can cost per pier look misleading?

One proposal may include engineering, excavation, restoration or lifting that another proposal excludes. Unit-price arithmetic is only one part of the comparison.

What if contractors recommend different pier counts?

Ask each contractor to show the affected area, support locations and evidence behind the count. If major structural proposals conflict, an independent professional evaluation may help clarify the diagnosis.
