2026 EXCAVATION COST GUIDE

How much does exterior foundation excavation cost?

Excavating beside an existing home is usually more involved than digging a shallow utility trench. HomeGuide's 2026 cost data lists digging around an existing house at roughly $5,000 to $15,000, while general residential excavation can be lower when the project is smaller or easier to access.

Why exterior foundation excavation gets expensive

  • Depth: exposing a basement wall may require substantially deeper excavation than surface drainage work.
  • Access: fences, neighboring structures, decks and narrow side yards can limit equipment size and increase labor.
  • Soil: rock, dense clay, wet soil or unstable material can slow digging and complicate backfill.
  • Spoil handling: excavated soil may need temporary storage, haul-away or replacement with better drainage material.
  • Restoration: landscaping, patios, walkways, driveways and grading may need to be rebuilt after the trench is closed.

Excavation is often only one line in a larger project

The trench may be needed to install exterior waterproofing, repair a foundation wall, add drainage, replace damaged materials or access utilities. That means the excavation price should be separated from membranes, drains, wall repair and final restoration whenever possible.

For related scopes, compare our foundation drainage repair cost guide, foundation waterproofing cost and foundation French drain cost guide.

Questions to ask before approving the excavation

  • How many linear feet of foundation will be exposed?
  • What depth and trench width are included?
  • Is soil haul-away included?
  • What backfill material will be used?
  • Who restores grading, landscaping and hardscape?
  • Are utility locating, permits or engineering included?

2026 source notes

HomeGuide's 2026 excavation guide lists typical total excavation around $1,100 to $5,600 for general residential work and about $5,000 to $15,000 for digging around an existing house. Angi lists general residential excavation around $1,658 to $6,707 and notes that access, labor and soil can materially change the total.
