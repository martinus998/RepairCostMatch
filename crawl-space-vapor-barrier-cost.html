2026 CRAWL SPACE GUIDE

How much does a crawl space vapor barrier cost?

A vapor barrier project can be a relatively simple ground-cover installation or part of a much larger moisture-control system. That difference is why two bids can look very different even when both use the phrase “vapor barrier.”

What changes the scope

  • Square footage: more exposed ground and wall area means more material and labor.
  • Material thickness: heavier reinforced liners generally cost more than basic polyethylene.
  • Sealing details: taped seams, piers, posts and wall transitions add labor.
  • Access: low clearance, tight entries, ducts and plumbing can slow installation.
  • Cleanup: debris, wet insulation or old plastic may need removal before new material goes down.

Vapor barrier versus encapsulation

A basic ground barrier mainly reduces moisture coming from exposed soil. Encapsulation usually goes further by lining walls, sealing vents and penetrations and sometimes adding dehumidification. If a contractor is proposing the larger system, compare our crawl space encapsulation cost guide.

Do not ignore standing water

A liner does not replace drainage. If water collects under the house, ask where it comes from and how it will be removed. Our crawl space moisture repair cost guide helps separate drainage, sump, dehumidification and structural scopes.

Questions to ask before approving the bid

  • What material thickness and reinforcement are included?
  • Will the liner cover only the ground or also the walls?
  • How are seams, posts and penetrations sealed?
  • Is old material and debris removal included?
  • Is drainage or dehumidification being proposed separately?

Structural damage is a different project

If moisture has already damaged joists, beams, sill plates or supports, ask for that repair to be priced separately. A vapor barrier can help manage moisture but does not restore damaged structural wood.

This guide is for planning only. The correct moisture-control system depends on the source of water, crawl-space conditions and local building requirements.
