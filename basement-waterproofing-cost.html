How much does basement waterproofing cost?

Basement waterproofing has one of the widest price ranges in home repair because “waterproofing” can mean anything from sealing a small leak to excavating around the foundation and installing a full drainage and membrane system.

Current 2026 consumer guides show very different averages depending on the work included. Angi reports professional projects commonly around the low-thousands to roughly $8,000, while other guides that model full 1,000-square-foot systems can run much higher. The right planning number depends on the water source and the method used.

Common waterproofing approaches

  • Crack sealing and coatings: generally used for limited moisture or specific entry points and usually sit toward the lower end of project pricing.
  • Interior drainage: drain tile, channel systems and sump pumps manage water after it reaches the foundation perimeter.
  • Exterior waterproofing: excavation, membranes and exterior drainage attempt to stop water before it enters the structure and are usually more labor-intensive.
  • Grading and downspout correction: moving surface water away from the house can be part of the solution when drainage is the main cause.
  • Crawl-space moisture systems: encapsulation, drainage and dehumidification have their own pricing structure.

What drives the price

Basement size, excavation access, foundation material, soil conditions, depth below grade, drainage layout, sump requirements, existing finishes and mold or water-damage repairs can all change the project. Exterior work often costs more because it requires excavation and restoration around the home.

Water after rain vs. constant moisture

Water that appears mainly after heavy rain can point toward grading, gutters, hydrostatic pressure or drainage problems. Constant dampness may involve condensation, plumbing, groundwater or a combination of causes. An online tool cannot determine which one applies to your house.

Questions to ask a waterproofing contractor

  • Where do you believe the water is entering?
  • Why are you recommending interior, exterior or combined work?
  • Is the sump pump, discharge line and backup power included?
  • What happens to finished walls or flooring during the work?
  • What does the warranty cover, and what maintenance is required?
