2026 FRAMING REPAIR GUIDE

How much does rim joist repair cost?

Rim joist repair can be a small localized framing job or part of a much larger perimeter repair. The final scope depends on how much wood is damaged, what caused the damage and whether the sill plate, floor joists or wall framing are also affected.

What may be included in the repair

  • Localized replacement: a short damaged section may be removed and replaced when surrounding framing is sound.
  • Longer perimeter sections: widespread rot or pest damage can require staged structural work.
  • Sill plate repair: deterioration often occurs near the sill, which can add temporary support and labor.
  • Joist-end repair: floor joist ends can also be damaged where they meet the perimeter.
  • Moisture correction: leaks, drainage or crawl-space humidity should be addressed so new material stays dry.

Why access changes the cost

Tight crawl spaces, finished basements, insulation, ducts, wiring and plumbing can make the perimeter difficult to reach. Structural repair may also require shoring or temporary support before damaged framing can be removed safely.

Compare nearby framing separately

If the sill plate is damaged, see our sill plate repair cost guide. If floor joists are part of the scope, compare floor joist repair cost. This helps you see whether two contractor bids are repairing the same components.

Questions to ask before approving work

  • How many feet of rim joist are damaged?
  • Is the sill plate or any joist end also affected?
  • Will temporary structural support be needed?
  • What caused the damage?
  • Does the estimate include moisture correction, pest treatment and finish restoration?

When crawl-space moisture is part of the problem

Persistent humidity or water can damage multiple framing components. Use our crawl space moisture repair cost guide to separate water-control work from structural replacement.

This guide is for planning only. Structural safety and the correct repair method require an on-site evaluation.
