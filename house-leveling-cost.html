2026 LEVELING COST GUIDE

How much does house leveling cost?

“House leveling” can describe several very different repairs, which is why online prices often look inconsistent. A settled concrete section may need slab lifting, a crawl-space floor may need support adjustment, and a structurally settling foundation may require piers or underpinning.

For planning, 2026 public cost guides show localized concrete leveling commonly in the hundreds to low-thousands, while structural foundation leveling or lifting can rise into the several-thousand to tens-of-thousands range. The repair method matters more than one national average.

Common types of leveling work

  • Concrete or slab leveling: mudjacking or polyurethane foam may raise some sunken concrete sections when the underlying condition is suitable.
  • Crawl-space adjustment: supports, posts, beams or shims may be corrected when floors are uneven because of support conditions below.
  • Piering or underpinning: deeper supports may be installed when settlement is related to weak or moving soil.
  • Foundation raising: larger structural lifting projects can require synchronized jacking, temporary support and work on connected utilities.

Why estimates can differ dramatically

One contractor may be proposing to lift a slab while another is proposing to stabilize the foundation with piers. Those are not comparable scopes. Ask each contractor to show what measured condition they are correcting, which areas will be lifted or stabilized, and whether the goal is cosmetic leveling, structural stabilization or both.

If piers are involved, see our foundation repair cost per pier, helical pier cost and push pier cost guides.

Signs that leveling may be part of a larger foundation problem

Sloping floors, doors that stop fitting, separated trim, recurring cracks, visible settlement and movement across several rooms can justify a closer structural evaluation. A single low spot in a concrete slab is different from movement across the load-bearing structure.

Questions to ask before approving a leveling job

  • What measurements show where the structure is out of level?
  • Are you lifting, stabilizing, or both?
  • What is causing the movement?
  • How many support points or piers are included?
  • Are plumbing, electrical, engineering, permits and restoration included?
  • What amount of movement does the warranty cover?

2026 source notes

This Old House lists full foundation lifting and leveling near the high end of foundation-repair work, while HomeAdvisor and Angi show that localized concrete leveling can be far less expensive. That spread reflects different scopes, not a contradiction.
